About this site

A public record of the Philippines' major projects

Building the Philippines follows large national projects from announcement to completion, so Filipinos at home and abroad can see what has been promised, what has happened, and where the two differ.

How this site works

Checked weekly
Official sources are reviewed every week. Each item on a project's Where We Are page shows the date it was last checked.
Make up your own mind
The site sets out information and sources. It does not argue for or against any project, and reaches no conclusion on your behalf.
Primary sources first
Official records are used wherever they exist. Reporting is used only where no official record covers the story.
Sources
The bodies this site monitors for each project are listed on the page.
No contact, no comments
This is a reading resource, not a forum. There is no comment section and no way to get in touch here.
